## // ABOUT US

SAK University's official game development circle. We operate as a **small, output-driven engineering unit** — not a casual club.

Members work across game development, information security, AI, and embedded systems. The objective is concrete: **competitions, publications, and shipped projects**. We value verification over hype, and execution over meetings.

> **"Build. Break. Document. Repeat."**

## // CHARTER

<details>
<summary><strong>Membership & Activity</strong></summary>

- **Trial period:** First 1 week after joining is probationary.
- **Monthly requirement:** At least 1 activity per month (competition, study session, event, etc.) + activity report. Results are not judged.
- **Hiatus:** 2 consecutive months without activity → temporary hiatus; shared resource access suspended.
- **Return:** Simple re-confirmation restores active status.
- **Withdrawal:** Consult the Representative in advance.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Shared Resources</strong></summary>

- All shared resources (literature databases, e-books, online learning, member-created code/assets) are for **personal learning and research only**.
- **No third-party sharing or redistribution** of accounts, materials, or member-created resources under any circumstances.
- Violations may result in suspension of the entire circle's access.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Prohibited Conduct</strong></summary>

1. Cheating or plagiarism in competitions or papers.
2. Unauthorized use or external leakage of shared resources.
3. Harassment of any kind.
4. Any other behavior that significantly disrupts the circle.

**Enforcement:** Officers discuss → hear the member's explanation → graduated response: warning → suspension → forced withdrawal. Records are kept for handover.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Governance</strong></summary>

- **Officers:** Representative (1), Vice Representative (1), Development Lead (1).
- **General meeting:** Twice per semester (approx. every 3 months). Activity reports, financial reports, officer appointments, rule amendments.
- **Rule amendments:** Passed by majority vote of attendees at a general meeting.

</details>
